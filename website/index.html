Daedalus

Many people with Parkinson's know the moment when their feet stop and won't start again: in a doorway, turning around, starting to walk. Their own tricks fade, a laser cane stays at home, and outdoors there is often nothing at all. Daedalus puts help on the watch they already wear: one press, a beat at their own pace to step to, quietly, in public too.

A beat at your own pace

Press "I'm stuck" on your watch and a steady stepping beat starts, set to your own walking pace with your physiotherapist.

On the wrist or in your ears

Feel it on your wrist or hear it in your earbuds. It stops on its own once you're walking.

Your week, at a glance

See when you got stuck beside your doses and last night's sleep, and share it with your care team if you choose.

A moment to breathe

Guided breathing exercises on your watch, for a calm pause during the day.

Daedalus works on Apple Watch and Wear OS watches.

All of Daedalus is in development and not yet available. It will be offered after verification and validation testing. In the first version, sound in earbuds works with iPhone.

Who is helping develop it

Medicina's apps are being shaped with the people who live with these symptoms every day and those who care for them. Daedalus will be tried at home before it is offered more widely.

People with Parkinson's

They share what their symptoms are like day to day and try the app at home.

Carers and family

They share what helps on hard days and what they need to see to support someone.

Physiotherapists

They set each person's walking pace and bring what they know about cueing and falls.

Neurologists

They help decide what the journal shows about symptoms, doses and sleep between visits.

About Medicina

Medicina is a Canadian health startup based in Hamilton, Ontario, and part of Innovation Factory's Catalyst program.

Living with a chronic disease can be frustrating and isolating. Medicina starts with diseases that affect how people move, such as Parkinson's, because a wearable can sense movement and respond in the moment. Daedalus is for freezing, when the feet suddenly stick to the floor: one press on the watch starts a beat to step to.

Health information will be stored in Canada. Anyone who uses Daedalus will be able to delete their data and account from the app.
