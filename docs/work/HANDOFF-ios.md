## Choices where the brief was silent (with reasons)

- **`beat_start_ms` for earbuds is carried back to the watch, not clock-aligned afterwards.** The iPhone replies to the start message with when the first click reaches the ear, on its own clock: the play time plus `outputLatency + ioBufferDuration`, because Bluetooth adds roughly 0.1 to 0.25 s. It also sends its receive and reply times. The watch converts the start to its own clock with an NTP-style offset over that round trip (`Pilot.beatStartOnWatchClock`, tested). For the wrist, `beat_start_ms` is taken just before the first `WKInterfaceDevice.play`.
- **The first wrist pulse of a study press plays before the workout session and the recorder start.** Starting the workout held the main actor for about 0.7 s before the first tap in the simulator. Press to first pulse is now about 60 ms there. Cues outside study mode start exactly as before.
- **Earbuds availability is read from a cache, not asked for at the press.** Asking at the press would delay every press, wrist presses included, by a round trip. The watch asks the iPhone when the app comes to the front, and the iPhone pushes route changes. A value older than 5 minutes, or an unreachable iPhone, counts as "not connected". The draw then uses only the places known to be there.
- **During the ≤1 s wait for the iPhone, the wrist stays silent.** Playing the wrist meanwhile would mix both places and blur H3. After 1 s without confirmation the wrist plays and the press is recorded as a wrist press.
- **Earbuds lost mid-beat:** the wrist carries on, and the press keeps `place: "earbuds"` with its original start, because that is where the beat started. A press whose beat never started on the iPhone is recorded as a wrist press. The recorded `earbuds_connected` and `phone_carried` are the values used for the draw, so the draw can be recomputed from the file.
- **What counts as a press:** "I'm stuck" on the watch (home, tremor result, check-in). It doesn't cover cues started from the iPhone, or "I'm stuck" inside a guard (shadow mode, not part of the pilot; that flow is unchanged).
- **The 10-minute limit applies to every walking cue while study mode is on**, not only to presses.
- **The check-in counts against the 6 when it is shown**, whether or not it gets an answer. A new press while it is up closes it with `null`.
- **Pocket reminders use `WKApplicationRefreshBackgroundTask`.** watchOS decides the exact time (it is budgeted), so a reminder can arrive late. A local notification from the iPhone would arrive on time but can't check whether the phone walked along.
- **Seed default 0**, shown in the field. The physio should type the participant's seed.
- **`HKWorkoutSession` is unchanged.** A `WKExtendedRuntimeSession` of the physical-therapy type runs up to an hour in the background with motion and haptics, so it could carry a 10-minute study cue. Live heart rate would be lost, and the App Review risk (2.5.4) would go. Not switched, as the brief asked.

## Where the spec needs correcting (with platform docs)

1. **The pocket beat can't run on iPhone with the phone locked.**
   - Apple staff, Developer Forums thread 676629: "No, currently there is no way to run haptics in the background." `CHHapticEngine.StoppedReason.applicationSuspended` says the engine stops when the system suspends the app in the background.
   - So, as the plan foresaw, **the pocket place runs on Android phones only**, and iPhone participants get wrist and earbuds. `docs/product/plan.md` B9 should say so.
   - An untested option: `AudioServicesPlaySystemSound(kSystemSoundID_Vibrate)` from an app holding a background audio session. It is one fixed ~0.4 s buzz with no pace control, so it isn't the same cue.
2. **Apple Watch can't detect a stand-up in the background** without a running session (no background motion or barometer trigger). B10 on Apple Watch is the timed fallback the plan describes, with the timing caveat above.
3. **`CMSensorRecorder` records the accelerometer only**, at 50 Hz, for up to 12 h a call, kept 3 days, even while the app is suspended (Apple docs, `recordAccelerometer(forDuration:)`). The docs don't state how late the data becomes readable, hence the live-sample fill and the 90 s delay before writing.
4. **Null beat start and no-motion presses: settled by the rules added to the brief.**
   - The `pilot_analysis.py` on this branch (0812fae) crashes on `beat_start_ms: null` (`TypeError`) and on empty `samples` arrays (`IndexError`).
   - The updated copy on the Android branch (ca72dd9) accepts both: it keeps the check-in and leaves the timings empty.
   - The apps follow both rules:
     - `beat_start_ms` is null whenever no beat start was confirmed (Stop during the ≤1 s wait for the iPhone, or a late iPhone reply). It is never the press time.
     - A press with little or no motion is still an ordinary `press_<code>_<index>.json`.
   - Both are unit-tested and checked against ca72dd9 (see Validation).
   - This branch needs ca72dd9's `pilot_analysis.py` merged before analysis.
5. **"A reinstall that keeps the seed and the press counter repeats the same schedule"** (`pilot.py` `draw` docstring): on Apple Watch the press counter lives in the watch app's `UserDefaults`, which a reinstall erases. The counter then restarts at 0 and repeats early draws. If that matters, keep the counter on the iPhone too.

## Decisions this forced, for the owner

- **iPhone participants have two places, not three.** Within-person H12 (pocket against wrist) can only come from Android phone users. Do you recruit H12 from Galaxy Watch and Android phone users only, or give iPhone participants an Android phone to carry?
- **The iPhone app now declares the `audio` background mode and motion use** (`UIBackgroundModes`, `NSMotionUsageDescription`). That's fine for the ad hoc pilot build. For an App Store build without the pilot, remove them or justify them.
- **Earbuds presses can wait up to 1 s with no beat** if the iPhone doesn't answer. Shorter means more fallbacks to the wrist (fewer earbuds presses). Longer means a later beat. 1 s matches the plan's "clicks start within 1 s".
